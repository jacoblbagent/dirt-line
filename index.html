How to build a line

1 · Plan first

  • Click Plan (buttons, bottom centre) for the overhead view. Click the ground to drop lips, landings, rollers, tables, berms and dirt holes.
  • Wheel = radius · Shift+wheel = height · the ◀ ▶ buttons rotate the take-off heading.
  • RMB deletes a feature. Plan sheet keeps a live progress list with you while you dig.
  • Planned features show as ghost shapes in the world and on the minimap.

2 · Dig

  • LMB works the ground with the tool in hand. Spade cuts firm dirt, pick axe breaks hard or packed dirt, rake levels and grooms.
  • Dig anywhere you like — in-situ volume really leaves the ground.
  • Spoil goes in your hands (130 L), or into the wheelbarrow (300 L) when you are stood next to it.

3 · Place and pack

  • RMB places dirt exactly where you are looking. Wheel sets the radius — down to 0.22 m for precise shaping. Shift+RMB trickles it out.
  • With the barrow in reach, RMB tips that out instead of your hands.
  • Poured dirt is loose — it slumps under gravity to its angle of repose (~35°).
  • LMB with the flat head packs it — preferred, and leaves a smooth sheen. The back of the spade also slaps loose dirt down, leaving ridge marks.
  • Shift+LMB shaves a clean flat cut — lips and landing faces.
  • Work slowly and you can hold a real lip: dig → place → slap → shave → pack.

Reading the dirt

  • HUD shows packing %, face angle and whether it will slump.
  • Marks tell the story: smooth sheen = flat head, ridges = spade, grooves = rake, pocks = pick.
  • Everything else is a button on the bar at the bottom. Esc frees the mouse to click them.
