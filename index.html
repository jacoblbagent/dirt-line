PLAN MODE — lay out the line before you dig

How to build a line

1 · Plan first

  • P — overhead plan view. Click the ground to drop lips, landings, rollers, tables, berms and dirt holes.
  • Wheel = radius · Shift+wheel = height · Q/R rotate the take-off heading.
  • RMB deletes a feature. Tab opens the plan sheet — keep it up while you dig.
  • Planned features show as ghost rings and wireframes in the world and on the minimap.

2 · Dig

  • Spade digs soft ground. Pick axe breaks hard or already-packed dirt (and loosens it).
  • Dig anywhere you like — in-situ volume really leaves the ground.
  • Spoil goes in your hands (130 L), or into the wheelbarrow (B sets it down, 300 L).

3 · Place and pack

  • E toggles PLACE DIRT. Wheel controls the dump radius — down to 0.15 m for precise shaping.
  • LMB pours, RMB trickles, Shift feathers it. With the barrow within reach you empty that instead.
  • Poured dirt is loose — it slumps under gravity to its angle of repose (~35°).
  • Pack it with the flat head (preferred) or the back of the spade. Packed faces hold 60°+.
  • Shift+LMB with flat head / spade shaves a clean flat cut — lips and landings.
  • Rake levels and grooms, dragging high spots into hollows.
  • Work slowly and you can hold a real lip: dig → dump → slap → shave → pack.

Reading the dirt

  • HUD shows packing %, face angle and whether it will slump.
  • Marks tell the story: smooth sheen = flat head, ridges = spade, grooves = rake, pocks = pick.
